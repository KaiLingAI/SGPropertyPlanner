⚖Fixed vs Floating — Quick Guide
Fixed Rate
✓ Certainty — same payment every month
✓ Good when rates are rising
✗ Higher initial rate
✗ Penalty if you sell/refinance during lock-in
SORA Floating
✓ Lower rate when SORA is low
✓ No lock-in (some packages)
✗ Payment fluctuates with SORA
✗ Risk if SORA rises sharply
Kai Ling's take: For most buyers in 2026, a 2-year fixed gives the best balance of certainty and cost. SORA has been falling — floating may benefit from further cuts, but fixed gives peace of mind. Always review at the end of your lock-in period.
🔄Refinancing — When to Review
Review your mortgage 3 months before your lock-in period ends. Switching to a lower rate on a $1M loan can save $300–600/month.
Key checklist:
• Check your current lock-in expiry date
• Compare rates from at least 3 banks
• Factor in legal/valuation fees (~$2,000–3,500)
• Ensure remaining loan > $200K (otherwise savings may not justify cost)

⚡ABSD Remission Rules
SC couples upgrading from HDB: ABSD paid upfront, fully refunded if existing HDB resale is completed within 6 months of new private property purchase.

Decoupling: One spouse transfers their share to the other, freeing a "first property" slot to purchase a second property at 0% ABSD. Subject to TDSR individual qualification.

Developers (licensed housing developers): ABSD remittable under specific conditions.

Trusts: 65% ABSD applies regardless of beneficial owner's profile.
