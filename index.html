⚡ABSD Remission Rules
SC couples upgrading from HDB: ABSD paid upfront, fully refunded if existing HDB resale is completed within 6 months of new private property purchase.

Decoupling: One spouse transfers their share to the other, freeing a "first property" slot to purchase a second property at 0% ABSD. Subject to TDSR individual qualification.

Developers (licensed housing developers): ABSD remittable under specific conditions.

Trusts: 65% ABSD applies regardless of beneficial owner's profile.
