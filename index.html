🏠 HDB Concessionary Loan
✦ LTV: 75% of lower of price/valuation
✦ Rate: 2.6% p.a. (0.1% above CPF OA rate — fixed quarterly)
✦ Downpayment: 25% — all in cash or CPF OA
✦ Max tenure: 25 years
✦ No lock-in period — refinance to bank anytime
✦ Must use all CPF OA first (can retain $20,000)
✦ Once refinanced to bank — cannot return to HDB loan
Eligibility: SC only. Income ceiling $14,000/mo. Must not own private property.
🏦 Bank Loan
✦ LTV: 75% of lower of price/valuation (0 loans)
✦ Rate: Variable (SORA-pegged, fixed 2–3yr then floating)
✦ Downpayment: 25% — min 5% in cash, 20% cash or CPF
✦ Max tenure: 30 years (HDB/EC)
✦ Lock-in: typically 2–3 years (penalty ~1.5% of loan)
✦ Flexible CPF usage — choose how much CPF to apply
✦ Can refinance to another bank after lock-in
Available to SC, PR, foreigners (subject to TDSR/MSR). No income ceiling.

📤Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD) — Residential Property Updated 4 Jul 2025
SSD is payable when you sell a residential property within the holding period after purchase. It applies to the higher of selling price or market value. HDB owners are exempt due to the MOP requirement.

⚖Fixed vs Floating — Quick Guide 2026
🔒 Choose Fixed if...
✦ You want predictable monthly payments
✦ You're a first-time buyer on a tight budget
✦ You believe SORA will rise back above 2% in 2–3 years
✦ Fixed costs ~0.10–0.20% more than floating now — a small premium for certainty
〜 Choose Floating if...
✦ You expect SORA to stay low or fall further to ~1%
✦ You plan to sell or refinance within 2 years
✦ You can absorb small monthly payment variability
✦ On $500K loan: floating saves ~$50–80/mo vs fixed right now
💡 2026 Context: Bank fixed rates (1.45–1.80%) are now below the HDB Concessionary Rate of 2.60%. This is why HDB-to-bank switches surged 7× at OCBC in 2025. However, once you switch from HDB loan to bank — you cannot go back. Make sure you are comfortable with variable rates or lock-in penalties before switching.
